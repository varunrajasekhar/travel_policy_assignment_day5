# Travel Exceptions and Approvals

Policy exceptions must be approved before the expense is incurred whenever reasonably possible.

A manager may approve documented exceptions for lodging, meals, or ground transportation.
Business-class airfare requires Vice President approval and must also meet the flight-duration rule in the Airfare Policy.

Emergency exceptions should be documented as soon as practical after the traveler is safe and able to report the situation.

When the policy does not contain enough information to answer a question, the employee should contact Corporate Travel rather than assume approval.
