# Lodging Policy

Employees should choose a safe, business-appropriate hotel near the work location.

The standard nightly lodging cap is $220 before taxes.
For designated high-cost cities, including New York City, San Francisco, Boston, and Washington DC, the nightly cap is $325 before taxes.

If no compliant hotel is reasonably available, the employee must document the business reason and obtain manager approval before booking above the cap.
Personal minibar charges, movies, and leisure upgrades are not reimbursable.
