# Airfare Policy

Employees should book the lowest logical fare that meets the business schedule.

Economy class is required for domestic travel and for international flight segments under 8 hours.
Premium economy is permitted for an international flight segment of 8 hours or more.
Business class requires Vice President approval and is allowed only for an international flight segment of 10 hours or more.

Airfare should be booked through the company's approved travel portal whenever the route is available there.
Change fees caused by a personal preference are not reimbursable.
