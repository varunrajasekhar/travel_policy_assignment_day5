# Ground Transportation Policy

Use the most reasonable transportation option for the location and schedule.

Public transportation is preferred when safe and practical.
Rideshare or taxi is reimbursable when public transportation is impractical, unavailable, or would materially increase travel time.

Rental cars require a business need such as multiple work sites, transporting equipment, or a location without reasonable local transportation.
Luxury vehicle upgrades are not reimbursable.
Parking and tolls incurred for business travel are reimbursable with receipts when available.
